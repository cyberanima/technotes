3. <u>```YOUR``` Laptop Topic Settings</u>:

## 3. ESP32S3 MQTT Clients  
Refer to the article -- [MicroPython – MQTT Publish/Subscribe using ESP32/ESP8266](https://www.donskytech.com/micropython-mqtt-esp32-esp8266/).   

```Assessment 3``` (WiFI: Your mobile ```Hotspot```)  
- Host: ```YOUR``` IP Address  
- Port: 1883  
1. Assure that Mosquitto is running on your laptop. Program your ESP32S3 to generate a random temperature value and publish it to the topic -- ```temperatue```; meanwhile, subscribe to the same topic and print the temperature value to the shell.  
2. Use your mobile to subscribe to the topic -- ```temperature```.  

```Assessment 4``` (WiFi: Your mobile ```Hotspot```)  
- Host: ```YOUR``` IP Address  
- Port: 1883  
1. Program ```YOUR``` ESP32S3 to generate the random temperature values and publish it to the topic -- ```temperatue```; meanwhile, subscribe to the topic -- ```humidity```, which is published by your ```PARTNER's``` ESP32S3, and print the humidity value to the shell.  
2. Your ```PARTNER's``` ESP32S3 generates the random humidity values and publish it to the topic -- ```humidity```; meanwhile, subscribes to the ```temperature``` topic and print the temperature value to the shell.  
3. Use your mobile to subscribe to all of the two topics.

```Assessment 5``` (WiFi: Your mobile ```Hotspot```)  
- Host: ```YOUR``` IP Address  
- Port: 1883  
1. Connect DHT11 to your ESP32S3 and program it to read the temperature and humidity values and publish them to the topics -- ```env```; meanwhile, subscribe to the same topic and print the temperature and humidity values to the shell.
2. Use your mobile to subscribe to the topic -- ```env```.
